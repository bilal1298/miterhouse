Painting the exterior of a house is one of the highest-impact home improvements you can make. It protects the structure from weather damage and immediately changes how the house looks from the street. For a typical 2,000-square-foot home, professional exterior painting costs $4,000 to $9,000, while a well-executed DIY job runs $800 to $1,500 in materials and equipment rental.

Here is exactly where that money goes and what drives the cost up or down.

## Cost Per Square Foot by Siding Material

Not all exterior surfaces cost the same to paint. The siding material determines prep difficulty, paint consumption, and application method.

| Siding Material | Cost Per Sq Ft (Pro) | Paint Coverage | Prep Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smooth wood (clapboard) | $2.00 - $4.00 | 350-400 sq ft/gal | Medium to heavy |
| Rough-sawn wood | $2.50 - $5.00 | 200-300 sq ft/gal | Medium |
| Vinyl | $1.50 - $3.00 | 400+ sq ft/gal | Light |
| Aluminum | $1.50 - $3.50 | 400+ sq ft/gal | Light to medium |
| Stucco | $2.00 - $4.50 | 150-250 sq ft/gal | Medium |
| Brick | $2.50 - $5.00 | 100-200 sq ft/gal | Medium to heavy |
| Fiber cement (HardiPlank) | $1.50 - $3.50 | 300-400 sq ft/gal | Light |
| Concrete block | $2.00 - $4.00 | 100-200 sq ft/gal | Medium |

Paint coverage varies with surface texture. A smooth surface like vinyl uses less paint per square foot than a porous surface like brick. This directly affects material costs.

## Labour: The Biggest Line Item

Labour makes up 65 to 80 percent of the total exterior painting cost. Here is why.

### Prep Labour

Surface preparation alone accounts for 50 to 70 percent of total labour hours on most exterior paint jobs. This is the work that determines whether the paint lasts 5 years or 12 years.

**Power washing:** 2 to 4 hours for a typical home. Essential for removing dirt, mildew, and loose paint. Professional power washing alone costs $200 to $500.

**Scraping:** 4 to 20 hours depending on condition. Every square inch of loose, peeling, or flaking paint must be removed. This is tedious, physically demanding work.

**Sanding:** 2 to 8 hours. After scraping, feathering the edges creates a smooth transition between painted and bare surfaces.

**Caulking:** 2 to 6 hours. Every gap, crack, and joint needs sealing. This prevents water infiltration behind the paint, which causes peeling from the back side.

**Priming:** 3 to 6 hours. Bare wood, patches, and stain bleed-through all require primer before the finish coat.

**Wood repair:** Variable. Rotten fascia boards, damaged siding, and deteriorated window trim should be repaired before painting. Minor repairs ($200 to $500) can be handled by the painting crew. Major carpentry ($500 to $3,000+) may need a separate contractor.

### Application Labour

The actual painting is faster than the prep, especially if the crew uses an airless sprayer for the body and back-brushes for adhesion.

**Spraying:** 4 to 8 hours for the body color (two coats) on a 2,000-square-foot home
**Brush and roller for trim:** 6 to 12 hours depending on the amount of trim, windows, and detail work
**Second-story access:** Adds 20 to 40 percent to application time due to ladder repositioning, scaffolding setup, and reduced productivity at height

### The Total Labour Math

For a typical 2,000-square-foot, two-story home in fair condition:

| Phase | Hours | Labour Cost ($40-$55/hr) |
|---|---|---|
| Power washing | 3 | $120 - $165 |
| Scraping and sanding | 10 | $400 - $550 |
| Caulking | 4 | $160 - $220 |
| Priming | 4 | $160 - $220 |
| Masking and protection | 3 | $120 - $165 |
| Body painting (2 coats) | 8 | $320 - $440 |
| Trim painting | 8 | $320 - $440 |
| Cleanup | 2 | $80 - $110 |
| **Total** | **42** | **$1,680 - $2,310** |

Most professional crews of 2 to 3 painters complete this scope in 4 to 6 working days.

## Material Costs

Paint quality matters more on exteriors than interiors because exterior paint faces UV radiation, temperature cycling, moisture, and physical weathering. Cheap paint fails faster.

### Paint

**Premium exterior paint** (Sherwin-Williams Duration, Benjamin Moore Aura, PPG Timeless): $50 to $80 per gallon. These offer superior adhesion, UV resistance, and colour retention. Expected lifespan: 8 to 12 years.

**Mid-range exterior paint** (Sherwin-Williams SuperPaint, Benjamin Moore Regal, Behr Ultra): $35 to $55 per gallon. Good performance for most applications. Expected lifespan: 6 to 8 years.

**Budget exterior paint** (builder-grade or store brand): $25 to $35 per gallon. Adequate for low-visibility areas or rental properties. Expected lifespan: 3 to 5 years.

**How much paint you need:** Measure the exterior wall area (height times perimeter, minus window and door openings). Divide by the paint's coverage rate (typically 350 to 400 square feet per gallon for smooth surfaces). Multiply by 2 (for two coats). Then add 10 percent for waste and touch-ups.

For a 2,000-square-foot home: approximately 10 to 15 gallons for the body and 2 to 4 gallons for trim. At premium prices, that is $600 to $1,200 for paint alone.

### Primer

Separate primer is needed for bare wood, heavy colour changes, stain bleed-through, and previously unpainted surfaces. Budget $30 to $50 per gallon for quality exterior primer.

A self-priming paint (paint-and-primer-in-one) works for repaints over sound, existing paint in a similar colour. Do not rely on it over bare wood or dramatic colour changes.

## Colour Change Costs

Changing the exterior colour is not just a style decision --- it affects the project cost.

**Similar colour (light to light, dark to dark):** Standard pricing. Two coats of finish paint.

**Light to dark:** Two coats of tinted primer plus two coats of finish paint. Add 30 to 40 percent to paint material costs and 20 percent to labour.

**Dark to light:** This is the expensive one. Dark pigments bleed through light paint aggressively. Expect two coats of high-hide primer (like a shellac-based primer) plus two coats of finish paint. Add 50 to 60 percent to paint material costs and 30 percent to labour.

The EPA's [Renovation, Repair and Painting program](https://www.epa.gov/lead/renovation-repair-and-painting-program) requires additional safety measures if your home was built before 1978 and any paint disturbance is involved. This can add $500 to $2,000 to the project for lead-safe work practices.

## What Affects Your Specific Quote

Beyond the standard factors, these property-specific conditions move your quote higher or lower.

**Number of stories.** Each additional story adds 25 to 40 percent to the cost. Ladder and scaffolding work is slower and more dangerous.

**Architectural complexity.** Lots of trim, multiple gable ends, dormers, bay windows, and decorative elements increase cut-in work dramatically.

**Condition of existing paint.** A house that was last painted 5 years ago and is in good shape costs half to paint compared to a house where the paint is peeling everywhere.

**Landscaping interference.** Dense foundation plantings, trellises, and items that need covering add setup time.

**Access limitations.** Narrow side yards, fences close to the house, and decks or additions that block ladder placement all reduce crew efficiency.

## Getting and Comparing Quotes

Ask every potential painter to bid on the same scope. Provide:

1. Your home's approximate square footage and number of stories
2. Siding material
3. Whether you want trim included
4. Current paint condition (good, fair, or poor)
5. Whether you are changing colour
6. Any known issues (rotten wood, peeling, mildew)

Compare quotes on total price, scope (is prep included? trim? caulking?), paint brand and product specified, warranty terms, and timeline.

## Is Exterior Painting Worth It?

From a property value standpoint, exterior painting is one of the best returns in home improvement. The fresh-paint look has an outsized effect on curb appeal and perceived home value. Realtors consistently rank it among the top five cosmetic improvements for resale.

From a structural standpoint, paint is your home's first defense against moisture, UV damage, and insect infiltration. Deferring exterior paint maintenance accelerates wood rot, which turns a $5,000 paint job into a $15,000 paint-plus-siding-repair job. Stay on schedule, and the paint protects everything underneath it.
