Drywall repair in Arlington runs $200 to $400 for a basic patch — 20–40% above the national average thanks to Northern Virginia's high labor rates and a contractor pool that stays booked 2–4 weeks out. Add EPA lead compliance costs for pre-1978 homes, and a simple crack repair that costs $175 elsewhere starts at $250 here.

Here's the full cost picture and what makes the Arlington market different.

## Arlington Repair Costs vs. National Average

| Repair Type | National Average | Arlington Market |
|---|---|---|
| Nail pop or small hole | $100–$200 | $125–$250 |
| Crack repair (under 4 ft) | $125–$275 | $175–$350 |
| Hole patch (fist-sized) | $150–$300 | $200–$400 |
| Water damage section | $300–$800 | $400–$1,000 |
| Full room retexture | $500–$1,800 | $800–$2,500 |

Most Arlington drywall contractors have a minimum service charge of $200 to $350. Group your repairs — three nail pops and a small crack should be one service call, not four.

## Why Arlington Costs More

**Labor rates reflect the market.** Northern Virginia's cost of living drives skilled trade wages higher than the national median. A drywall finisher earning $25/hour in Richmond commands $35–$45 in Arlington.

**Demand outpaces supply.** Good drywall contractors in the Arlington corridor stay busy year-round. The 2–4 week scheduling window for non-emergency work is normal, not an anomaly.

**Pre-1978 compliance adds cost.** Any contractor disturbing painted surfaces in homes built before 1978 must hold EPA RRP (Renovation, Repair, and Painting) certification under [federal law](https://www.epa.gov/lead). That certification costs the contractor money, and the containment procedures add time to every job.

## Arlington Housing Stock and Your Walls

The repair approach changes based on what's behind the surface — and Arlington's housing mix is unusually diverse.

**Plaster walls (pre-1950s homes in Lyon Park, Cherrydale):** Not drywall at all. Repair requires setting-type compound or specialized plaster mixes, and the walls are rarely flat. Budget 20–40% more than standard drywall repair.

**Plaster skim over drywall (1960s–70s homes):** The base is drywall finished with a thin plaster coat. Matching that texture on a patch takes more skill than matching standard drywall finish.

**Standard drywall (1980s–present):** Half-inch on walls, 5/8-inch on ceilings for fire rating. The most predictable to repair.

## Common Arlington-Specific Problems

### Settlement Cracks in Established Neighborhoods

Homes in Cherrydale, Clarendon, and Lyon Village develop diagonal cracks at window and door corners as decades of settling takes its toll. Most are cosmetic. But recurring cracks in the same spot may signal foundation movement worth investigating before you patch again. A [drywall specialist](/blog/drywall-professional-repairs-a-homeowners-guide/) can tell the difference.

### Humidity Damage

Arlington summers are brutal on interior walls. Basements and bathrooms in older homes without adequate ventilation develop bubbling tape joints, mold behind baseboards, and soft spots. Fix the moisture source before touching the drywall — otherwise you're paying for the same repair twice. If [water stains](/blog/water-stains-on-drywall-honest-advice-for-your-home/) have appeared, address the leak first.

### Townhome Party Walls

Fairlington, Courtbridge, and Dominion Hills townhomes share walls with neighbors. Some owners add a second layer of drywall for soundproofing, which changes the approach for future repairs. Make sure your contractor knows what's behind the surface before quoting.

### Popcorn Ceilings

Many 1970s and 1980s Arlington homes still have popcorn ceilings. If yours was applied before 1980, test for asbestos before disturbing it — a test kit costs $25 to $40, or hire a certified inspector for $200 to $400.

## Finding and Vetting Arlington Contractors

**Where to look:** Neighborhood listservs (Lyon Park, Bluemont, and Ashton Heights all have active email lists with contractor recommendations), NextDoor reviews specific to your area, and Virginia's contractor license lookup.

**What to verify:**
- Virginia contractor license (required for work over $1,000)
- General liability insurance certificate
- EPA RRP certification for pre-1978 homes
- References from Arlington jobs specifically — someone who works primarily in Loudoun County may not understand Fairlington townhome quirks

**Red flags:** No physical business address, full payment demanded upfront, no references from the past six months, or a quote significantly cheaper than three competitors. That last one usually means unlicensed, uninsured, or planning to skip finishing coats.

Get three written quotes. Compare them against the cost table above to spot outliers.

## DIY in Arlington: When It Makes Sense

For nail pops, small holes, and hairline cracks, the DIY approach saves $150–$300 per service call. The process:

1. **Assess.** Push on surrounding drywall — if it flexes or feels soft, the damage extends beyond what's visible.
2. **Prep.** Remove loose material, old tape, or crumbling compound. Cut back to solid drywall.
3. **Patch.** Self-adhesive mesh for holes under 6 inches. For larger holes, cut a clean rectangle, install backing, and fit a matching piece.
4. **Mud.** Three thin coats of joint compound, each feathered wider than the last. Thin beats thick every time.
5. **Sand.** 150-grit between coats. Light pressure — aggressive sanding digs grooves.
6. **Prime.** PVA primer evens out absorption so the repair doesn't flash through paint.

For [ceiling work](/blog/drywall-ceiling-repair-cost-breakdown-real-numbers/), [water damage](/blog/drywall-flood-cut-honest-advice-for-your-home/), or texture matching on visible surfaces, hire a [professional](/blog/drywall-repair-diy-or-call-a-pro/). The finishing skills that make a repair invisible take years to develop.

## Seasonal Timing

- **Spring and fall** are ideal — moderate temperatures and humidity help compound dry at the right rate.
- **Summer** humidity stretches dry times. Run a dehumidifier and allow extra hours between coats.
- **Winter** works fine indoors if the room stays above 55°F. Joint compound won't cure properly in cold conditions.

## Paint Matching After Repairs

Even with the original paint can, the color on your wall has shifted from sun exposure and aging. Bring a chip of existing paint (cut from an inconspicuous spot) to a Benjamin Moore or Sherwin-Williams store for color matching.

For [ceiling repairs](/blog/drywall-ceiling-repair-cost-breakdown-real-numbers/), matching is harder because ceiling whites yellow over time. Repainting the entire ceiling is often the only way to avoid a visible patch — budget accordingly.
